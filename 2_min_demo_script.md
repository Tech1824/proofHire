**Format:** Fast-paced screen recording with voiceover

## SCENE 1: The Problem & The Start (0:00 – 0:15)
**Page:** `#jobs` (Open Roles)  
**Action:** Pan mouse over the backend job card.

> **Voiceover:** 
> "Today’s hiring is optimized for impressions, not evidence. Candidates use AI to polish resumes and pass coding tests. ProofHire fixes this by turning a candidate’s actual project code into a structured, AI-driven interview."

---

## SCENE 2: The Graph Context (0:15 – 0:35)
**Page:** `#graph` (Knowledge Map)  
**Action:** Click 'Graph' in sidebar. Let it animate. Highlight a node.

> **Voiceover:** 
> "We don’t just dump code into an LLM. We ingest their GitHub and profile claims into a local knowledge graph. Every file, function, and skill is mapped. When the AI generates a question, it uses focused graph retrieval—keeping token costs tiny and context highly accurate."

---

## SCENE 3: The Depth Check (0:35 – 1:15)
**Page:** `#depth` (Depth Check)  
**Action:** Click 'Depth check' in sidebar. Scroll down to show the chat and the Evidence Panel on the right (Score, Quotes, Dimensions). 

> **Voiceover:** 
> "The core of ProofHire is the Depth Check. The AI interrogates the candidate strictly on the code they submitted. When they answer, the system scores them in real-time on understanding, reasoning, and—most importantly—Ownership.
> 
> If a candidate used AI to write their code but doesn't actually understand it, their Ownership score tanks. We also built a voice-to-voice pipeline, allowing real-time, async verbal interviews powered by Gemini Flash."

---

## SCENE 4: Ranked Evidence (1:15 – 1:40)
**Page:** `#shortlist` (Shortlist)  
**Action:** Click 'Shortlist' in sidebar. Expand the 'Evidence quotes' section on Arjun Mehta's card to show the blockquotes.

> **Voiceover:** 
> "Finally, candidates are ranked strictly by verified evidence. Recruiters don’t have to trust a black-box AI score. They can expand any candidate to read the exact quotes that justified the score, linked straight back to the conversation. It’s fully auditable."

---

## SCENE 5: Conclusion (1:40 – 1:55)
**Page:** Back to `#jobs` or leave on `#shortlist`. 
**Action:** Keep mouse still.

> **Voiceover:** 
> "ProofHire runs entirely on your own infrastructure. Candidate data stays private. It stops script-kiddies, respects senior engineers' time, and gives recruiters actual proof of skill. 
> 
> The signal is in the explanation."
