ProofHire company portal
========================
1. Keep all files and the css/ and js/ folders together.
2. Open login.html (or index.html) and click "Continue in demo mode".
3. Use the sidebar to move between pages.

Pages: login, dashboard, company-profile, jobs, applicants, candidate,
assessments, ai-report, compare, shortlist, graph (Knowledge graph).
Demo data lives at the top of js/portal.js. Data is saved in the browser (localStorage).
